# Social_Media_Stock_Analysis
An interactive Tableau dashboard analyzing 5 years of stock  market performance of 4 major social media companies —  Meta, Pinterest, Snap and Spotify

#Overview
*The rapid growth of social media platforms has made them key players in the stock market. 
*This project explores how Meta, Pinterest, Snap and Spotify performed over 5 years from 2021 to 2026 — analyzing price trends, trading volume, growth patterns, risk and volatility through interactive Tableau dashboards.

#Features
*Dual dashboard layout covering price analysis and comparative analysis
*Interactive candlestick chart with open-close color coding
*Sparkline KPI cards for opening, closing, high and low prices
*Comparative growth performance line chart
*Trading volume analysis line chart
*Risk vs return scatter plot with quadrant analysis
*Yearly price volatility stacked bar chart
*Company filter buttons for stock specific analysis
*Year and date range filters applied across all charts
*6 slide Tableau Story for guided presentation

#Key Metrics
*Open Price — Stock price at market open
*Close Price — Stock price at market close
*High Price — Highest price of the trading day
*Low Price — Lowest price of the trading day
*Volume — Number of shares traded per day
*Avg Daily Return % — Average daily price change
*Positive Return Days % — Days stock closed higher
*Negative Return Days % — Days stock closed lower
*Avg Daily Volume — Average shares traded per day
*Risk (Std Dev) — Price standard deviation as risk measure
*Volatility — Yearly price standard deviation by stock

#Key Insights
*Meta and Spotify delivered the strongest 5 year growth
*Strong bull run observed from 2023 to 2024 followed by sharp market decline in 2026
*Snap recorded the highest trading volume spike in 2026
*Snap showed worst risk return balance — highest risk with lowest return
*Meta showed best risk return balance — strong returns with relatively lower risk
*Positive and negative return days almost equal at 50.11% vs 49.24% showing high market uncertainty
*Overall market volatility increased significantly after 2024 across all stocks

#Technical Aspects
*Tool — Tableau Desktop
*Data Format — Microsoft Excel (.xlsx)
*Data Connection — 4 sheets unioned in Tableau
